Safe-to-queue trades no longer bypass those gates or enumerate unsearched captures. A conservative material exchange check rejects obvious queen donations. Plausible recaptures retain the high attempt probability; the two ignored attempts limit and smooth queued/reactive input remain covered.

Coalescing only overdue intermediate motion samples keeps the original path and timing, destination, press/release edges, and per-dispatch guards. The strength card displays the effective opponent/persona-derived Elo; its editor still edits the configured target.
